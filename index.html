Privacy Policy

Fruit Cutter Game for Android

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Everything stays on your phone

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • The camera is optional. If you switch on hand control, the game looks at the live camera picture to find your hand. It never saves, records or sends that picture anywhere.
  • Your best score and your sound settings are saved on your phone only.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.

About this policy

This policy explains what information Fruit Cutter Game (“the app”) uses when you play. The app is published by Your Name (“we”, “us”). It applies only to this app.

What the app uses

Information Why the app uses it Where it goes
Live camera picture (only when hand control is on) To find your hand, so you can cut fruit by moving it. Read on your phone in real time. Not saved, not shared, not uploaded.
Best score To show your high score on the menu. Saved on your phone only.
Sound and music settings To remember whether you turned them on or off. Saved on your phone only.

Camera permission

The app asks for the camera permission only so you can control the game with your hand. You can say no and still play with your finger.

  • The hand-recognition model (Google MediaPipe) is built into the app and runs on your phone. It does not need an internet connection.
  • The app does not take photos or record video, and it does not keep any camera frames. Each picture is used once to find your hand and is then discarded.
  • You can take the permission back at any time in Android Settings, under Apps, then Fruit Cutter Game, then Permissions.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store or receive on any server:

  • your name, email address or phone number
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, videos or files
  • audio from your microphone
  • advertising identifiers or any other tracking data

The app contains no advertising or analytics code, and it has no accounts.

Third parties

We do not sell or share personal information, because the app does not collect any.

  • Google MediaPipe is the hand-tracking library inside the app. It runs on your phone and does not send data to us or to Google.
  • Google Play delivers the app. If you have turned on usage and diagnostics sharing on your phone, Google may collect technical information such as crash reports under Google’s own privacy policy, and may give us summaries of it. We do not control that collection.

Storage, backup and security

Your best score and settings are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot normally read.

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include this app’s saved data in your Google backup, so your best score can return when you set up a new phone. Google and your device settings control that backup. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, write to us at lilpsychologist410@gmail.com.

Your choices

  • Play with touch only by not allowing the camera permission.
  • Turn sound and music off inside the app.
  • Delete your saved score and settings by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what information it uses, for example by adding ads, analytics or online leaderboards, we will update this page and its date before that version is released.
